Per-Meal Average Time

Live-recomputed from real Start Time → Inventory "Added to Shopify" timestamps.

"Added to Shopify" is often a bulk push, not a per-batch stamp — several batches can share one timestamp because they were verified together, not because they finished at the same minute. That bundles queue-wait time into the recorded duration, and it hits small batches hardest (a fast small batch can look artificially slow sitting in the same bulk push as a big one). Treat this table as directionally right, not to-the-minute precise — especially for meals near the top of the "slowest" list with small average batch sizes.
